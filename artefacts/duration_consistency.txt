============================================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR B3
============================================================================================

  B3 compares the WRITTEN rule ('>10 spikes per minute') against the
  EXECUTED one (>=100 spikes). The executed rule used whatever duration
  meaRtools believed the recording was. So the number that decides B3
  is meaRtools' rec.time, NOT the true wall-clock recording length.

  If rec.time is the spike-time span, it differs per recording, which
  means the effective spike-count criterion ALSO differs per recording
  -- and that is a sharper finding than the off-by-one ever was.
