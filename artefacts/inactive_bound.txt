==============================================================================
WHAT THIS SETTLES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
==============================================================================

  SETTLED (A7a, the substantive question):
    The dataset's own record of electrodes 'not recorded due to
    malfunctioning electrodes during acquisition' is bounded near zero
    at every timepoint on both plates. Whatever else happens to these
    cultures over 66 days, the experimenters did not record electrodes
    dropping out of the hardware.

  NOT SETTLED (A7b, the bookkeeping question):
    Whether the list was regenerated per recording or carried forward.
    A bound of ~0 makes the question nearly moot -- there is almost
    nothing in the list to carry forward either way -- but it is not a
    positive answer and should not be written as one.

  The bound is an UPPER bound. A recorded electrode with zero detected
  spikes is indistinguishable from an unrecorded one by this method.
  Given A5b -- the detector always returns something -- zero-spike
  electrodes are vanishingly rare, which is why the bound is tight.
